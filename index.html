Privacy Policy

Our bots help communities manage their servers. This policy explains what operational information may be processed, why logs are used, and how we handle personal information.

Effective: 10 October 2026Independently operated · Solo owner
Privacy commitment: We do not intentionally publish personal information. If personal information is identified in bot-controlled storage or memory, we aim to remove it as soon as reasonably possible. This policy describes intended practices; actual collection and deletion depend on each bot's code, configuration, and hosting systems.

01. Information our bots process

Our bots may collect and process server logs and activity information, including:

  • Commands used, command details, timestamps, and results
  • Server events, actions, moderation activity, and bot interactions
  • Technical details needed to understand events, such as server, channel, role, or user IDs and usernames where included in logs
  • Errors, diagnostics, configuration, and security-related events

The exact information depends on the bot features, permissions, and server configuration. Operational logs can sometimes include identifiers or text submitted as part of a command.

02. Why we use logs

Logs are used to operate and maintain the bots, review command activity, troubleshoot errors, monitor server-related events, support moderation and management features, protect against misuse, and improve reliability.

We do not intentionally post personal information publicly or use logs to expose individual users. Access to bot logs should be limited to the operator and any server staff who can access logging channels configured by that server.

03. Personal information and deletion

We do not intend to maintain personal information as public profiles or share it with the public. If personal information is found to have been captured in bot-controlled storage or active memory, we aim to delete it as soon as reasonably possible and remove it from the bot's memory.

Some logs may temporarily contain Discord IDs, usernames, command text, or other information associated with an event. Deleting information from backups, Discord channels, hosting providers, or other third-party systems may require separate action and may be subject to their controls.

04. Storage and retention

Depending on how a bot is configured, operational information may appear in runtime memory, console output, log channels, files, databases, or hosting services. We aim to retain information only as long as reasonably needed for operation, security, and troubleshooting.

We cannot promise that every log is automatically deleted immediately unless the relevant bot and hosting setup are configured to do so. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information to bot commands.

05. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information or intentionally share it publicly. We do not knowingly disclose personal information to third parties except where needed to operate a bot, respond to a lawful request, protect services, or address abuse.

Discord and any hosting, database, or integration providers used to run the bots may process information as part of their services under their own privacy policies. Server administrators may be able to view information posted to log channels they control.

06. Your privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about information a bot has processed or request deletion of personal information under our control. We will review the request and take appropriate steps as soon as reasonably possible.

For information stored by a Discord server, Discord itself, or another provider, you may need to contact the relevant server administrators or service directly.

07. Security and policy changes

As a solo-operated bot group, we take reasonable steps to restrict access to bot systems and operational logs. No online service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

This policy may be updated if our bots, logging practices, or services change. The effective date at the top will be updated when we make material changes.
